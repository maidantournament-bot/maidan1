Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024 · MAIDAN respects your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: Name, email, mobile number, username
  • Payment info: Transaction IDs, payment screenshots (for verification only)
  • Game info: In-game usernames and IDs you provide
  • Usage data: Pages visited, challenges posted, match history
  • Device info: Browser type, IP address (for security)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and process transactions
  • To verify deposits and withdrawals manually
  • To resolve disputes and investigate fraud
  • To send important notifications (challenge updates, payments)
  • To improve the platform

3. Data Storage

Your account data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and on our secure servers. Payment screenshots are used only for verification and stored securely. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Payment Screenshots

Payment screenshots you submit are used solely to verify your deposit. They are reviewed by our admin team and stored securely. Unverified screenshots are deleted after 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • eSewa – payment processing
  • Khalti – payment processing
  • Binance – cryptocurrency payments
  • Google Fonts – typography

6. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Update or correct your information
